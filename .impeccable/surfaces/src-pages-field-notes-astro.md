# Field notes and articles
Mode: Read. Primary action: choose a useful note, read it, then find another note or return to the gear.

Preserve the daylight ground, upright Barlow headings, forest photograph and ordinary text links. Keep the index as an unboxed reading list, not a product-card grid. Articles have a clear back link, a single title, a quieter introductory paragraph and a comfortable 68ch measure; headings divide the practical steps without decorative numbering. Related reading precedes the restrained blue shopping invitation. Native ordered/bulleted lists retain their conventional markers and spacing between long items; inline reading links use underlined store ink, inherit the visible focus ring and stay in the same tab.

All article titles, text and excerpts come from published EmDash Pages, never a second storefront content list. Source seeds create new notes only; subsequent merchant edits are authoritative. Optional related-reading failures do not hide the article. Index failures show an error and HTTP503, not a false empty state.
