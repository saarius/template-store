# Store search
Mode: Operate. Find an item, collection or field note and open its canonical page. Preserve EmDash search, Commerce product visibility and safe snippet rendering; no indexed price is shopping authority.

## Direction contract
THESIS: One labeled GET form, useful destinations, honest empty/error states; remove the inherited developer showcase and duplicate live-search box.
OWN-WORLD: Existing daylight ground, blue ink, Barlow headings and yellow primary action. Ordinary links and quiet horizontal rules, no cards or decorative chrome.
STORY: Search by a familiar word, distinguish gear from collections and reading, then follow a result or browse all gear.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Store navigation, search title and short explanation, one full-width field/action row, then grouped results. On phones the button can sit below the field. The signature is the plainly named destination groups, not an animation.
FORM: Narrow refinement of the existing store search, inside the established brand; no visual-world or open composition round. Empty, too-short, zero-result and unavailable states remain distinct.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
