## Owner Steer 7 — November 20, 2026 (Supersedes Steer 3 `@clawsweeper re-review` push trigger)

### ClawSweeper Re-Review Rule (from Ronald, 18 Nov)
- Every push to a PR starts a ClawSweeper review by itself. **Do not post `@clawsweeper re-review` after you push.** That queues a second review of the same commit and delays the result.
- Post `@clawsweeper re-review` **only** when:
  - the review on your latest commit ended in an error, or
  - about 10 minutes after a push, there is still no "ClawSweeper picked this up" comment for that commit.
- Post it **once per commit**, and **never** while a review is queued or running.
